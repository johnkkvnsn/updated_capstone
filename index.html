Modernizing Local Governance Finance

A comprehensive system proposed to centralize financial recording, monitoring, and reporting processes for both barangay and SK into a single platform.

Discover the Project

About The Project

Addressing the challenges of manual financial systems.

Currently, many barangays and SK offices still rely on manual bookkeeping processes, which may lead to inefficiencies, errors, and delays in financial reporting.

By implementing a Barangay Financial Management Streamlining System, this study aims to modernize financial processes, improve bookkeeping practices, and strengthen financial management for both barangay and SK, ultimately contributing to more transparent and efficient governance at the local level.

Core Features

Designed for efficiency, accuracy, and transparency.

Automated Reporting

Built-in templates for financial statements, ensuring faster, standardized, and accurate report preparation.

Real-Time Monitoring

Track budgets, expenditures, and fund allocations in real time, improving transparency and decision-making.

Centralized Bookkeeping

Ensures accurate and organized documentation of financial activities in a single platform.
