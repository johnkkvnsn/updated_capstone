SK Generate Reports

Generate Daily, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly SK financial reports — figures are computed automatically from your SK income & expense records.

 All SK reports follow the official templates from SK Paule 1, Rizal, Laguna. Data is auto-computed from recorded SK transactions.

SK Cashbook
Chronological ledger of all SK collections and disbursements with running balance — Daily, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly.
SK Disbursement Voucher
Official SK DV with payee info, particulars, tax deductions (3% Non-VAT, 1% Withholding), and three-party certification block.
SK Abstract of Quotations
Price comparison table for up to 3 suppliers with SK Councilor and Chairperson signature block — matches Paule 1 template.
SK Liquidation Report
Liquidation of SK fund utilization for a Daily/Monthly/Quarterly/Yearly period — approved disbursements only.
SK Financial Statement
Statement of SK fund position — beginning balance, income, disbursements, and ending cash balance for any period.
Fund Status & Budget Utilization
Summary of total budget, income, expenses, net balance and allocation utilization for SK or Barangay funds.
