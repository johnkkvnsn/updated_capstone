Generate Financial Reports

Select a report type, fill in the required details, and generate your official financial report.

 All reports follow the official COA/DBM-prescribed BFDP/BFR Annex formats. Figures are auto-computed from your recorded transactions.

BFR-1: Daily Collections & Disbursements
Itemized register of collections and disbursements for a single day. Switch to Monthly/Quarterly/Yearly/Custom anytime from the form.
BFR-2: Actual Income & Expenditure
Statement of actual income and expenditure for past fiscal year — Part A (Income) and Part B (Expenditures by PS/MOOE/CO). BFDP Form No. 2.
BFR-3: 20% Component of NTA
Priority development projects funded from the 20% NTA utilization with ranking and cumulative totals. AIP Form No. 4.
BFR-4: Annual Procurement Plan
Annual procurement plan with item description, total cost, and target quarter of implementation.
BFR-5: List of Notices of Award
Quarterly list of awarded contracts — date, project, procurement type, supplier, and amount.
BFR-6: Monthly Collections & Disbursements
Itemized two-column register of all collections and disbursements for a chosen Daily/Monthly/Quarterly period.
BFR-7: Statement of Receipts & Expenditures
Official annual statement of receipts & expenditures — local sources, IRA, MOOE, PS, CO. Annex B, JMC 2018-1.
Fund Status & Budget Utilization
Summary of total budget, income, expenses, net balance and allocation utilization.
Purchase Request
Request form for items/services to be procured — quantity, unit, description, unit cost, and purpose.
Canvass
Comparative price canvass among suppliers for a requested purchase, prior to the formal Abstract of Quotations.
Abstract of Quotations
Formal comparison of supplier quotations with BAC member certification and recommendation of the lowest calculated bid.
Purchase Order
Formal order issued to the winning supplier — items, unit cost, delivery and payment terms.
Notice of Award
Formal notice to the winning supplier confirming acceptance of their quotation and the awarded contract amount.
Inspection & Acceptance Report
Certifies that delivered items were inspected and accepted against the Purchase Order.
Disbursement Voucher
Authorizes payment to a supplier/payee, with budget, cash, and legality certifications plus payment/check details.
